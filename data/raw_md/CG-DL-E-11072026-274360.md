का.आ. 3778(अ).— केंद्रीय सरकार, आप्रवास और जविेजियों जवषयक अजधजनयम, 2025 (2025 का 13) की 
धारा 4 की उप-धारा (1) द्वारा प्रित्त िजियों का प्रयोग करते हुए, एतद्द्वारा भारत सरकार, गृह मंत्रालय की अजधसूचना, िो

"39.  भोगापुरम (आंध्र प्रिेि)”.

MINISTRY OF HOME AFFAIRS 
NOTIFICATION  
New Delhi, the 10th July, 2026 
S.O. 3778(E).— In exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section (1) of section 4 of the 
Immigration and Foreigners Act, 2025 (13 of 2025), the Central Government hereby makes the following 
amendments in the notification of the Government of India, Ministry of Home Affairs, published in the 
Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II, Section 3, Sub-section (ii) vide number S.O.3987(E), dated the 1st 
September, 2025, namely:-  
In the said notification, in the Table, in category I. AIRPORTS, after serial number 38 and the entries 
relating thereto, the following serial number and entry shall be inserted, namely:- 
 “39.  Bhogapuram (Andhra Pradesh)”. 
 
 
  [F.No. 25022/61/2025-Imm] 
NITESH KUMAR VYAS, Addl. Secy. 
 
Note: The principal notification was published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II, Section 3, Sub-
section (ii), dated the 1st September, 2025 vide number S.O. 3987 (E), dated the 1st September, 2025 and 
amendment issued vide notification S.O. 3478 (E) dated 29.06.2026.
