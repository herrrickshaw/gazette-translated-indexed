MINISTRY OF TEXTILES 
ORDER 
New Delhi, the 27th October, 2026 
S.O. 5794(E).— In exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section (1) of section 3 of the Jute Packaging 
Materials (Compulsory Use in Packing Commodities) Act, 1987 (10 of 1987), the Central Government hereby makes 
the following amendment in the Order of the Government of India in the Ministry of Textiles, published in the 
Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II, Section 3, sub-Section (ii) vide number S.O. 1830(E), dated the 22nd April, 
2025, namely:- 
 
In the said Order, in the Preamble, in paragraph 4, for the words, figures and letters “up to the 30th June, 
2025”, the words, figures and letters “up to the 31st January, 2027 or until further orders, whichever is earlier” shall be 
substituted. 
[F. No. J-7/1/2024-Jute Section] 
PADMINI SINGLA, Jt. Secy. 
 
 
Note: The principal Order was published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II, Section 3, Sub-section (ii) vide 
number S.O. 1830(E), dated the 22nd April, 2025.
