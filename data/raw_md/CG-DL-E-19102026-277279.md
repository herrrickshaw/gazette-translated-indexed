MINISTRY OF MINES 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 16th October, 2026 
G.S.R. 898(E).— In exercise of the powers conferred by section 13 of the Mines and Minerals (Development 
and Regulation) Act, 1957 (67 of 1957), the Central Government hereby makes the following rules further to amend 
the Mineral (Auction) Rules, 2015, namely: — 
1. Short title and commencement:— (1) These rules may be called the Mineral (Auction) Third Amendment Rules, 
2026. 
(2) They shall come into force on the date of their publication in the Official Gazette. 
2.      In the Mineral (Auction) Rules, 2015 (hereinafter referred to as the said rules), in rule 9, — 
(a) in sub-rule (8), — 
(i) after the first proviso, the following proviso shall be inserted, namely: — 
“Provided further that, in case of auction of mineral specified in Part D of the First Schedule to the Act, 
the number of technically qualified bidders is between two and five, then all of them shall be considered as 
qualified bidders:”; 
(ii) in the second proviso, for the words “Provided further”, the words “Provided also” shall be substituted; 
(b) after sub-rule (9), the following proviso shall be inserted, namely: — 
“Provided that, in case of auction of mineral specified in Part D of the First Schedule to the Act, the 
total number of technically qualified bidders is two or more, the auction process shall proceed to the second 
round of auction in the manner provided in clauses (i) to (iii).”;  
(c) after sub-rule (10), the following proviso shall be inserted, namely: — 
“Provided that, in case of auction of mineral specified in Part D of the First Schedule to the Act, the 
total number of technically qualified bidders is less than two, then no technically qualified bidder shall be 
considered to be qualified bidder and the first attempt of auction shall be annulled.”. 
3.  In the said rules, in rule 19E, — 
(a) in sub-rule (9), in the first proviso, for the word “three”, the word “two” shall be substituted; 
(b) in sub-rule (10), for the word “three”, the word “two” shall be substituted; 
(c) in sub-rule (11), for the word “three”, the word “two” shall be substituted.
