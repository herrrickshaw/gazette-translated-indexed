NOTIFICATION  
New Delhi, the 9th October, 2025 
 
S.O. 4659(E).— In exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section (1) of Section 33 of the School of Planning 
and Architecture Act, 2014 (37 of 2014), the Central Government hereby makes the following amendments in the 
notification of the Government of India, erstwhile Ministry of Human Resource Development (Department of Higher 
Education), published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part-II, Section 3, Sub-section (ii) vide number  
S. O. 3239(E), dated the 3rd July, 2018, namely:- 
2.         In the said notification, for serial numbers 19, 20, 21 and the entries relating thereto, the following shall be 
substituted, namely:- 
“ 19. Prof. Kavita Daryani Rao, former Vice Chancellor, Jawaharlal Nehru Architecture and Fine Arts 
University, Hyderabad; 
Member;   
  
  20. Prof. Chetan Vaidya, former Director, School of Planning and Architecture, New Delhi; 
Member;   
  21. Prof. Manoranjan Parida, Director, CSIR-Central Road Research Institute, New Delhi; 
Member;   
”. 
[F. No. 3-7/2015-TS.VI] 
SAUMYA GUPTA, Jt. Secy.  
  
Note: The principal notification was published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II, Section 3, Sub-section 
(ii) vide number S.O. 3239(E) dated the 3rd July, 2018 and subsequently amended vide notification number 
S.O.5378(E), dated the 11th December, 2024.
