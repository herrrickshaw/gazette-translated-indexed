का.आ. 5660(अ).—िबदक, भारत सरकार के नागर जिमानन मंत्रालय की अजधसूचना संख्या सा.का.जन. 137(अ), 
दिनांक 17 फरिरी, 2026, िो भारत के रािपत्र, असाधारण, भाग II, खण्ड 3, उप-खण्ड (ii), दिनांक 20 फरिरी, 2026 में 
प्रकाजित हुई थी, और भारतीय िायुयान अजधजनयम, 2024 (2024 का 16) की धारा 10, 11, 19, 20, 32 और 33 द्वारा 
प्रित्त िजियों का प्रयोग करते हुए, िायुयान (खतरनाक माल का िहन) जनयम, 2026 बनाए गए थे; 
अब, इसजलए, िायुयान (खतरनाक माल का िहन) जनयम, 2026 के साथ पठित, भारतीय िायुयान अजधजनयम, 
2024 (2024 का 16) की धारा 19 तथा धारा 21 द्वारा प्रित्त िजियों का प्रयोग करते हुए, केंद्रीय सरकार एतद्द्वारा 
प्राजधकृत करती है: 
(i) 
दक जनयम 3 के उप-जनयम (2) के प्रथम परंतुक के अधीन इसके द्वारा प्रयोग की िाने िाली िजियों का 
प्रयोग महाजनिेिक, नागर जिमानन, संयुि महाजनिेिक, नागर जिमानन तथा जनिेिक (जिजनयम एिं 
सूचना)- खतरनाक माल प्रभाग द्वारा भी दकया िाएगा;

MINISTRY OF CIVIL AVIATION 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 15th October, 2026 
S.O. 5660(E).—Whereas, by the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry of Civil Aviation, 
vide G.S.R. 137(E), dated the 17th February, 2026, published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II, Section 3, 
Sub-section (ii), dated the 20th February, 2026 and issued in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 10, 11,19, 
20, 32 and 33 of the Bharatiya Vayuyan Adhiniyam, 2024 (16 of 2024), the Aircraft (Carriage of Dangerous Goods) 
Rules, 2026 were made;  
Now, therefore, in exercise of the powers conferred by section 21 and section 19 of the Bharatiya Vayuyan 
Adhiniyam, 2024 (16 of 2024), read with the Aircraft (Carriage of Dangerous Goods) Rules, 2026, the Central 
Government hereby authorizes:—  
(i) 
that the powers exercisable by it under first proviso to sub-rule (2) of rule 3, shall also be 
exercisable by the Director General of Civil Aviation, Joint Director General of Civil Aviation and 
Director (Regulations & Information)- Dangerous Goods Division; 
(ii) 
that the powers exercisable by the Director General of Civil Aviation under third proviso to sub-rule 
(2) of rule 3, shall also be exercisable by the Joint Director General of Civil Aviation and Director 
(Regulations & Information)- Dangerous Goods Division;
