MINISTRY OF WOMEN AND CHILD DEVELOPMENT 
NOTIFICATION  
New Delhi, the 8th June, 2026 
 
S.O. 4423(E).— In exercise of the powers conferred by Section 5 of the Charitable Endowments Act, 1890 
(6 of 1890), and on an application made by the Board of Management of the National Children’s Fund, the Central 
Government hereby makes the following further amendments in the notification of the Government of India in the 
then Department of Social Welfare No. S.O. 120(E), dated the 2nd March, 1979 (hereinafter referred to as the said 
notification) and as subsequently amended from time to time namely: 
In Schedule ‘B’ to the said notification— 
(a) for paragraph 2, the following paragraph shall be substituted, namely: 
 “The Objective of the Fund shall extend to the whole of India.”  
(2) for paragraph 3, the following paragraph shall be substituted, namely: 
“ For the management and administration of the Funds of the Fund, a Board of Management (hereinafter referred to as 
the ‘Board’) shall be constituted consisting of the following members, namely: 
i. 
Hon’ble Minister, Ministry of Women and Child Development – Chairperson (Ex-officio) 
ii. 
Hon’ble Minister of State, Ministry of Women and Child Development – Special Invitee (Ex-officio) 
iii. 
Secretary, Ministry of Women and Child Development – Working Chairperson 
iv. 
Joint Secretary in-charge of SPNIWCD in Ministry of Women and Child Development – Member 
v. 
Financial Adviser, Ministry of Women and Child Development – Member 
vi. 
Chairperson, National Commission for Protection of Child Rights (NCPCR) – Member
