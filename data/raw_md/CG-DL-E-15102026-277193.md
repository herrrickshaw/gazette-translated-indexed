MINISTRY OF LABOUR AND EMPLOYMENT 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 15th October, 2026 
 
G.S.R. 890(E).— In exercise of the powers conferred by clause (b) of sub-section (1) of section 15 
of the Code on Social Security, 2020 (36 of 2020), the Central Government hereby makes the following 
amendments in the Employees’ Pension Scheme, 2026, namely:- 
In the said Scheme, in paragraph 4, in sub-paragraph (2),- 
(i) 
for the words “such sum as may be specified by the Central Government”, the words “wages 
upto rupees fifteen thousand (Rs. 15,000/-) per month” shall be substituted;  
(ii) 
in the first proviso, the words “and the Central Government” shall be omitted.  
2. 
This notification shall come into force on the 17th September, 2026. 
 
[F. No. R-15025/01/2026-SS-II]
