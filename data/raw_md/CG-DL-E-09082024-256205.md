MINISTRY OF EDUCATION 
(Department of Higher Education) 
NOTIFICATION 
New Delhi, the 8th August, 2024 
 
S.O. 3211(E).—In exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section (1) of section 15 of the Architects Act, 
1972 (20 of 1972), the Central Government, after consultation with the Council, hereby makes the following 
amendments further to amend the notification of the Government of India in the erstwhile Ministry of Education and 
Social Welfare, number G.S.R. 68(E), dated the 21st February, 1973, published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, 
Part-II, Section 3, Sub-section (i), dated the 21st February, 1973, namely:— 
 In the said notification,- 
(a)  under the heading “U.S.A.”, in serial number 8(b), after entry number 36, the following entry shall be 
inserted, namely:—  
“37. Master of Architecture 2, awarded by the Southern California Institute of Architecture (SCI-Arc), 
Los Angeles, USA.”; 
(b)  under the heading “UAE” the serial number 16 shall be renumbered as “16(1)” thereof and after serial 
number 16(1) as so renumbered, the following entry shall be inserted, namely;- 
“(2) Bachelor of Architecture awarded by the American University in Dubai, UAE.” 
 2.        This notification shall come into force on the date of its publication in the Official Gazette.  
 
[F. No. 4-32/2021-TS.VI] 
 SAUMYA GUPTA, Jt. Secy.
